Welcome

A green pause in a busy city.

Tembusu Grove is a retreat concept named after one of Singapore’s best-loved heritage trees. Low buildings sit among lawns and tropical planting, so every room looks out onto green. Come for a long weekend, a family break or a quiet reset between journeys.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Anything else? Message us on WhatsApp and a real person will reply.

How do I book?

Send us your dates and number of guests on WhatsApp. We reply with available rooms and current rates, then confirm your booking in writing.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 pm and check-out is until 12:00 pm. Early check-in and late check-out are offered when rooms allow.

Is the retreat suitable for children?

Yes. The Family Garden Room and Canopy Suite suit families, and cots and children’s amenities are available on request.

Can you arrange transport from the airport?

Yes. Private transfers can be arranged on request; share your flight details on WhatsApp and we will confirm the rate in advance.

Do I need to book spa treatments in advance?

We recommend it, especially at weekends. Ask on WhatsApp and we will hold a time for you.

Is breakfast included?

Rates are available with or without breakfast. We will explain the options clearly before you confirm.

Your room in the garden is waiting.

Tell us your dates on WhatsApp and we’ll reply with rooms, rates and availability.
